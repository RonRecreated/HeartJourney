1. Why does this document exist?
    - To define an area of growth that can be assessed, taught, and encouraged throughout one or more journeys.
2. Who edits it?
    - Content administrators.
3. Who reads it?
    - Indirectly. Users will see its name and description, but they'll primarily experience it through reflection prompts and insights.

Every Reflection contributes towards a Dimension. Here are some:
- Communication
- Conflict Resolution
- Spiritual Growth
- Trust
- Boundaries
- Commitment
- Servanthood
- Forgiveness
- Emotional Health
- Leadership
- Wisdom & Discernment
- Purity

Each one would contain:
- Name
- Description
- Icon
- Color
- Display Order
- Scripture Theme (optional)

Reflection Prompts would reference one or more Dimensions, and Insights could also reference them. If you later decide to introduce a new dimension like "Financial Stewardship" in another journey, you won't have to change the underlying architecture.
