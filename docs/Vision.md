Heart Journey helps people grow in Christ-centered relationships through biblical truth, thoughtful reflection, and practical next steps.

Heart Journey is to always sound...
- truthful
- biblical
- gracious
- hopeful
- encouraging

Three Pillars of Heart Journey
1. Truth - Everything should be biblically grounded.
2. Grace - Truth without grace discourages.
3. Growth - Truth and Grace shouldn't be without Growth. The Goal is transformation.

Heart Journey uses structured reflection choices to help users discern their current condition, identify areas of concern, and take wise next steps. Optional notes exist for journaling, not for evaluation.
